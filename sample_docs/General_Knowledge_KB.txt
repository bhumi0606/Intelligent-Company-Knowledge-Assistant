GENERAL KNOWLEDGE AGENT - COMPANY REFERENCE DOCUMENT
Document Owner: Corporate Communications | Version 1.1 | Last Updated: 20 July 2026

This document covers general company information that does not fall under HR, IT Support, or
Finance. It is used by the General Knowledge Agent to answer miscellaneous employee queries.

1. COMPANY OVERVIEW
--------------------
Acme Corp was founded in 2011 and is headquartered in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India, with regional
offices in Bengaluru, Pune, and a satellite office in Singapore. The company operates in the
enterprise SaaS space, providing workflow automation tools to mid-market and enterprise clients
across 30+ countries. As of the last fiscal year, the company employs approximately 2,400 people
globally.

2. OFFICE FACILITIES
---------------------
The Ahmedabad headquarters includes a cafeteria (open 8:00 AM - 9:00 PM), a wellness room for
prayer/meditation/nursing mothers, a gym on the 2nd floor (badge access required, open 6:00 AM -
10:00 PM), and free shuttle service covering major residential routes in the city, with pickup
points and timings published on the internal Facilities portal.

Visitor policy: all guests must be registered at least 2 hours in advance via the Visitor
Management System and must be escorted by the hosting employee at all times within office premises.

3. COMPANY EVENTS AND CULTURE
-------------------------------
Acme Corp hosts a company-wide town hall on the last Friday of every month, streamed live and
recorded for employees who cannot attend. Annual events include the Founders' Day celebration in
March and the year-end Acme Awards Night in December, recognizing outstanding contributions across
departments.

Employee Resource Groups (ERGs) currently active include Women@Acme, PrideAtAcme, and the Acme
Green Initiative (sustainability-focused). Any employee can join an ERG by signing up on the
internal Community portal; no manager approval is required.

4. CODE OF CONDUCT AND GENERAL POLICIES
------------------------------------------
All employees are required to complete the Code of Conduct training annually, along with
Information Security Awareness and Anti-Harassment training. Non-completion by the assigned
deadline is escalated to the employee's manager and may affect the annual performance review.

Employees can report concerns about ethics, harassment, or misconduct confidentially through the
Ethics Hotline (ethics@acmecorp.com or the anonymous web form), which is managed independently of
HR and reviewed by the Ethics Committee.

5. MEETING ROOMS AND BOOKING
-------------------------------
Meeting rooms across all offices can be booked through the Outlook Room Finder or the internal
"BookIt" app. Rooms with video conferencing equipment are marked with a camera icon in the booking
system. No-show bookings (rooms reserved but unused for 15+ minutes) are automatically released for
other employees to book.

6. GENERAL QUERIES THAT DO NOT FIT ELSEWHERE
------------------------------------------------
- Building access badges are issued by Facilities on an employee's first day; lost badges should be
  reported immediately to Facilities and Security, with a replacement fee of INR 200 for negligent
  loss (waived for verified theft).
- Parking: limited on-site parking is available on a first-come basis; employees are encouraged to
  use the shuttle service or public transport where possible.
- The internal employee directory and org chart are accessible via the "PeopleHub" portal, searchable
  by name, team, or location.
- For queries that genuinely span multiple departments (e.g., "I'm relocating for a new role"),
  the General Knowledge Agent should direct the employee to both HR and Facilities as appropriate.

7. CONTACT
